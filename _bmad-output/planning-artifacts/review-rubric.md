# PRD Quality Review — Lesevenn

Reviewed 2026-09-25 against `prd-validation-checklist.md`, with `addendum-lesevenn.md` read alongside. Calibrated to the stated stakes: solo build, IBE160 (15 sp, Høgskolen i Molde), ~10 build weeks remaining, graded portfolio deliverable. Enterprise apparatus (sign-off, ROI, SLA, rollout) is correctly absent and is not treated as a gap anywhere below.

## Overall verdict

This is a genuinely good PRD, and unusually so for its stakes — it has a real thesis (§0: «ingen kvalitetspåstand teller i dette prosjektet med mindre den er målt mot et fast testsett»), it converts the brief's softest claim («relevant» oppfølgingsspørsmål) into four machine-checkable properties plus a counterfactual test, and it names its own worst failure mode out loud (FR-43: «at en Misoppfatning leses som Dekkende»). Decision-readiness, substance, coherence and shape fit are all strong; nothing here reads as furniture.

What is at risk is narrower and concentrated in two places. First, the measurement regime has holes exactly where the PRD's own logic says it should not: the correctness of Faguttrykk *explanations* — the single most-viewed piece of generated content, reused in Lesevisning and Øvekort — is never measured, while term *selection* is measured to two decimals; and FR-21's 60-case gold set exercises three of the five Svarvurdering states it is supposed to validate. Second, §4.11's density cap (FR-9) contradicts the Øvekort identity rule (FR-16) with no ranking basis defined, which an implementer must guess at. Third, on planning: the cut ladder in §9 stops at M2, leaving the heaviest block (M1) with no internal priority. Fix those four and this is safe to hand to architecture.

## Decision-readiness — strong

The PRD makes decisions and says they are decisions. §4.1 states the pivotal one in the open — mandatory review of Råtekst — and argues it against the alternative rather than asserting it: «en tekst med to spalter kan trekkes ut i feil leserekkefølge og fortsatt se ut som gyldig norsk». The addendum §4 then lists seven rejected alternatives *with what was given up*, including the honest ones: the closed language list is «mindre imponerende og mer sann»; additive display «koster skjermplass og er verdt den». That is trade-off disclosure, not balancing language.

§11 is the strongest single section. The open questions are actually open, and three are marked **Blokkerer** — including item 2, which points a loaded gun at the whole document: «Emnet er beskrevet med gruppevurdering, grupper på 4 ± 1. Dette prosjektet er planlagt som solobygg.» A PRD that leads its own premise risk rather than burying it has earned this verdict. §10's counter-metrics do the same work from the other side; SM-C4 («Språkmodellens egen vurdering av eget utdata … Skal aldri brukes som dokumentasjon») is the sharpest line in the document, because it forecloses the easiest way for the author to cheat himself.

Someone pushing back would find their objection acknowledged in nearly every case I tested. The friction objection to FR-3 is tagged as an assumption *and* raised as open question 6. The "are those the right three languages" objection is open question 5, with named alternatives (somali, tigrinja). The deadline objection is assumption 8 with the source's unreliability spelled out. The one thing missing is timing on the blockers.

### Findings
- **medium** Blocking open questions carry no resolve-by date (§11, items 1–3) — every milestone in §9 is dated to the day, but the three items that can void the plan are undated and unowned. Item 2 is described as «det viktigste punktet på listen, foran alt annet i dette dokumentet» and then sits ninth from the end of the document with no action attached. *Fix:* give each blocker a date (item 2 and 3 within the week; item 1 before M0) and one line on the branch — what §9 becomes if solo is refused, what §8 becomes if the proposal template forces a different scope.
- **low** No `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts anywhere in the document; one `[MERKNAD]` in §8.2. Not a defect on its own — §11 and the assumption register carry the load — but the two places where the PRD genuinely has *not* decided (FR-9's ranking basis, FR-27's scope) are written in the same settled prose as the places where it has. *Fix:* add callouts at those two spots so a reader can tell an argued decision from an unmade one.

## Substance over theater — strong

No findings. This dimension is where most PRDs at this stake level fail, and this one passes cleanly enough that flagging anything would be manufactured.

Three personas, all load-bearing. Eleven drives §4 top to bottom. Læreren gets exactly one job («Kunne peke elever til et verktøy de klarer å bruke selvstendig») and is then explicitly revoked in §2.2 — «Læreren er en talsperson i v1, ikke en bruker» — which is the opposite of persona theater: the PRD names a stakeholder and then refuses to let her generate requirements. Utvikleren is the unusual one and it earns its place, because the 70/30 portfolio weighting genuinely makes documentation a product requirement; §4.11 is downstream of that persona, not decoration.

The Vision (§1) could not swap into another PRD in this category. It is built on a specific negation — «Der et generisk KI-verktøy tar imot en tekst og returnerer et sammendrag — altså hopper rett til produktet av lesingen og gjør selve lesingen unødvendig» — and §7 converts that negation into an enforceable product rule: «Det finnes ingen «oppsummer teksten for meg»-knapp.» A vision statement that produces a non-goal is a vision statement doing work.

Differentiation is not claimed beyond what is defended. §4.6 calls the fagsamtale «Prosjektets sterkeste påstand, og derfor den som må måles hardest» and then measures it; §4.7 calls morsmålsstøtte «bevisst begrenset» and caps it at three verified languages rather than claiming coverage. The NFRs in §5 are mostly thresholds rather than adjectives (70-character line length, 8 % marking cap, two-second latency trigger) — the exceptions are recorded under done-ness below, not here, because they are omissions rather than boilerplate.

## Strategic coherence — strong

The thesis is stated in §0, repeated structurally, and the feature set follows it. Every quality claim in §4 lands on a named Gullsett with a number (FR-8, FR-14, FR-21, FR-28, FR-31), and §0 closes the loop economically: «Det er samme arbeid brukt to ganger — én gang for å gjøre appen god, én gang for å vise at den er det.» That is not a backlog with headings; it is one bet expressed twice.

Prioritization follows the thesis rather than ease. §9.1 puts gold-set annotation and the first measurement run in M0 with an explicit reason — «målingen styrer promptarbeidet, og en måling som først kommer i desember har ikke styrt noe» — which is the hard call, since measurement is the thing a solo student under time pressure would naturally defer. §9.4's code freeze on 30 November, five days before the working deadline, is the same discipline applied to the documentation deliverable. The cut order in §9.3 is argued from the pedagogical thesis, not from effort: Mapper and Minnevers go first because «begge er additive. Uten dem står det pedagogiske argumentet i §1 fortsatt.»

Success metrics validate the thesis rather than measuring activity. There is no DAU/MAU-style filler; SM-1 to SM-6 are pass/fail on named thresholds, and the four counter-metrics are real counter-metrics with named opponents — SM-C1 is explicitly «Motvekt til SM-2: gjenkalling kan alltid kjøpes ved å markere mer». MVP scope kind is problem-solving and the scope logic matches it. The one place the regime does not reach uniformly is Minnevers.

### Findings
- **medium** Minnevers sits outside the definition of done that the rest of the document is built on (§4.8, §9.5, §10) — FR-31 sets the strictest possible bar («antall usanne påstander i det som vises eleven er **null**»), but no SM in §10 references FR-30 or FR-31, and the eleven-step demo run in §9.5 never opens a Minnevers. §4.8 states the risk precisely — «et rim som stemmer dårlig faglig er verre enn ingen huskeregel, fordi eleven pugger noe galt i en form som sitter godt» — so the one feature that can make a student memorise something false is the one feature with no entry in the acceptance apparatus. *Fix:* either add a demo step (generate a vers, confirm every Faguttrykk resolves to the Begrepssett) and an SM-11 pointing at FR-31, or state in §10 that FR-31's threshold *is* the definition of done for §4.8 and that its omission from §9.5 is deliberate because Minnevers is last in the cut order.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate

At the FR level this is better than most commercial PRDs. «Konsekvenser (testbare)» is not a label pasted over prose — FR-4's table gives seven named failure states with a detection rule and a required user-visible action each, plus «Hver av PF-1 til PF-7 har en reproduserbar test med tilhørende testfil i repoet». FR-7 turns two vague quality wishes into machine checks with 100 % thresholds (verbatim substring, non-circularity). FR-19's five-state table specifies not just the classification but what the follow-up question must *do* per state. FR-9 gives two hard density ceilings. I looked for the usual weasels and found almost none: no "graceful", no "reasonable performance", no "user-friendly". §9.5 then does something rare — it defines done as an executable sequence («tre ganger etter hverandre, uten inngrep fra utvikleren») rather than a feeling.

The verdict is *adequate* rather than *strong* because of two holes that sit precisely where the PRD's own logic forbids them, plus one measurement-design error. The first is the explanation gap: FR-8's Gullsett annotates «hvilke uttrykk som *skulle* vært markert» — selection only. Nothing anywhere measures whether the explanation attached to a Faguttrykk is *true*. FR-7 requires it be ≤ 2 sentences, non-circular, and anchored to a Kildeavsnitt, but presence of a Kildeavsnitt is not faithfulness to it. Compare FR-31, which does demand «Ingen påstand i verset motsier Teksten» for the least critical feature in the app. The rhyme gets a fact-check; the definitions the student reads inline and then drills on flashcards do not.

The second is a live contradiction between FR-9 and FR-16, detailed below. The third is FR-21's gold-set composition. Beyond those, the non-functional bullets in §5 are the weakest-specified part of an otherwise ruthless document, and the two overlap thresholds (0,6) are unreproducible as written because the metric is never named — in a PRD whose entire thesis is reproducible measurement, that matters more than it normally would.

### Findings
- **high** Explanation correctness is never measured (FR-7, FR-8, and by inheritance FR-16, FR-27, FR-28) — the Begrepssett is the most-reused generated artefact in the product (Lesevisning markings, Øvekort, Minnevers source, translated glosses per §4.7), and its accuracy has no threshold anywhere. FR-8 measures only «Gjenkalling ≥ 0,75» and «Presisjon ≥ 0,70» on term selection. A run that scores 0,90/0,85 on selection while explaining «mitose» wrongly passes every gate in the document, and FR-16 then prints that explanation on a flashcard the student memorises before an exam. *Fix:* extend FR-8's existing nine-text Gullsett with a second manual judgement per extracted term — is the explanation consistent with its Kildeavsnitt, and is it comprehensible at ungdomstrinn level — with a threshold (e.g. ≥ 95 % faithful, zero contradictions of the Kildeavsnitt tolerated in what is shown). This costs no new texts, only a second annotation pass over material already being annotated.
- **high** FR-9's density cap contradicts FR-16, and the ranking it depends on does not exist (FR-7, FR-9, FR-11, FR-16) — FR-9 says «Overstiges taket, beholdes de høyest rangerte uttrykkene og resten kuttes», but FR-7 produces no rank or score field, §3's Begrepssett definition carries no ordering, and no FR defines what ranking means. Worse, it is unstated whether the cull removes terms *from the Begrepssett* or only from display. Both readings break something: culling the set collides with FR-16's «Øvekortsettet er identisk med Begrepssettet — ingen uttrykk lagt til, ingen fjernet» plus its avviksstest, while culling display only puts terms on flashcards that the student never met marked in the text — which contradicts §4.5's whole rationale, «at et uttrykk eleven møtte markert i teksten er det samme uttrykket hun øver på etterpå». An implementer cannot resolve this without guessing. *Fix:* decide it explicitly. Recommended: FR-7 emits a rank (e.g. model-assigned importance, tie-broken by first occurrence), the cull happens at extraction time so the stored Begrepssett is already within FR-9's cap, and FR-16's identity rule then holds trivially. Add the rank field to §3's Begrepssett definition so FR-11's Jaccard measurement is defined over the same set.
- **high** FR-21's Gullsett exercises three of the five states it validates (FR-19, FR-21, and SM-3 by inheritance) — the gold set is «fem Tekster × fire startspørsmål × tre svarvarianter (dekkende, delvis, misoppfatning)». Uklart and Utenfor never appear, so «Klassifiseringstreff … **Minst 75 prosent treff**» is measured over 60 % of the state space, and the confusion matrix cannot show the confusion the same bullet names as tolerable: «sammenblanding av Delvis og Uklart er langt mindre alvorlig enn å lese en Misoppfatning som Dekkende». FR-19's follow-up rules for Uklart («Be om utdyping av det *konkrete* uklare uttrykket») and Utenfor («Dele det opprinnelige spørsmålet i et enklere delspørsmål») are therefore unvalidated behaviours in a section whose premise is that relevance must be measured. These two are also the states a real struggling reader hits most — a one-word answer and a «vet ikke». *Fix:* add a small state-coverage set rather than doubling the matrix: 10 extra cases (5 uklart, 5 utenfor across the same five Tekster) scored for classification hit and follow-up-rule compliance only, not for the counterfactual test. Gold set becomes 70 cases; state the confusion matrix covers all five states.
- **medium** The two 0,6 overlap thresholds are unreproducible as specified (FR-20, FR-21) — FR-20 requires «ordoverlapp under 0,6 mot hvert tidligere spørsmål» and FR-21 «ordoverlapp under 0,6» for the counterfactual test, but neither names the measure, the tokenisation, or whether Norwegian inflection is normalised. FR-11 by contrast is precise: «minst 0,80 overlapp (Jaccard)». FR-20's sporbarhet check has the same problem — «minst ett innholdsord fra elevens svar» with «innholdsord» undefined and no stopword list, measured at «**100 prosent**». A 100 % threshold on an undefined predicate cannot be re-run, and FR-40 requires exactly that: «Målingene kan kjøres på nytt med én kommando». *Fix:* name the measure and the preprocessing once (e.g. Jaccard over lemmatised tokens minus a stopword list committed to the repo) in §3 or in FR-20, and have FR-21 refer to it.
- **medium** No latency ceiling or timeout/retry rule for the app's own model calls (§5, FR-7, FR-13, FR-19) — FR-2 bounds PDF extraction at 20 seconds with one retry, and FR-29 handles translation timeouts, but nothing bounds the calls on the critical path. §5's «Kall til språkmodell som tar over to sekunder viser en status som sier hva som gjøres» specifies when to show a spinner, not how long the student waits before the app gives up, and «eleven skal komme til aktiveringssiden raskt» is the one pure adjective in an otherwise numeric section. Svarvurdering is the latency-sensitive path — it happens mid-conversation, six times per Fagsamtale, on the strongest and slowest model per the addendum §3 — and it has no timeout, no retry rule, and no defined behaviour on failure. §5's «Ingen blindveier» promise is unfulfillable for the fagsamtale as written. *Fix:* add a §5 bullet with a per-call ceiling and failure behaviour (e.g. 30 s for generation of Begrepssett/Quiz, 20 s for Svarvurdering, one automatic retry, then a named error with the student's answer preserved and a retry button — the FR-2 pattern applied generally).
- **medium** Accessibility is the only requirement family stated as adjectives (§5) — «tilstrekkelig kontrast» and «forklaringen er tilgjengelig for skjermleser» are the sort of phrasing this PRD flags elsewhere, and they sit in a document that specifies eight percent, seventy characters and 0,80 Jaccard. This matters here more than it usually would, because §5 also says «Lesevisningen er den viktigste flaten» and the target users are struggling readers. §9.5 step 4 tests keyboard operation («én med tastatur alene») but nothing tests contrast or screen-reader output. *Fix:* name the numbers (WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for the marking indicator against its background), name one screen reader to test with, and add a §9.5 step that opens a Faguttrykk forklaring under it — accessibility is a defensible small deliverable when it is a checklist item and unverifiable when it is an adjective.
- **low** FR-13's coverage rule binds only for short texts — «ingen av Tekstens overskriftsdeler er uten minst ett spørsmål, når Teksten har fire eller færre deler». Texts with five or more heading sections have no coverage rule at all, which is most of the target material: UJ-1's «fire sider» chapter and UJ-4's five-part kapittel both land outside the guarded case. *Fix:* state the general rule (e.g. questions must span at least four distinct heading sections, or at least half the sections when there are eight or fewer) so the eight questions cannot cluster in the first page.

## Scope honesty — adequate

Omission discipline is genuinely strong, and the verdict is *adequate* only because the de-scoping ladder stops one rung above where it is needed.

Omissions are explicit at three levels and never left to inference. §2.2 names four classes of non-user, each with a reason — including the one most projects would quietly omit: «Elever uten noe norsk i det hele tatt.» §7 gives seven negations phrased as product rules, not disclaimers («Lesevenn er ikke en oversetter», «Lesevenn leser ikke bilder»). §8.2 gives seven out-of-scope items each with the reason it is out, and distinguishes credibility-based deferral from time-based deferral explicitly: «Utsatt av troverdighetsgrunner, ikke av tidsgrunner.» FR-4 even carries a per-FR «Utenfor dette FR-et» block. Nothing about this document leaves a reader inferring what was dropped.

The assumption register (§12) round-trips cleanly — I checked all ten: every inline `[ANTAKELSE]` has an index entry and every entry appears inline, in document order, at the sections cited. The tags are on real inferences rather than scattered defensively, and two of them carry their own falsification condition, which is the mark of an assumption someone intends to test: FR-28's «Går det ikke, må språket flyttes til Modellstøttet — en påstand uten kontroll er ikke kvalitetssikring», and §6.4's budget assumption, which is then used to justify four otherwise arbitrary caps.

Open-items density is proportionate: 8 open questions, 10 assumptions, 1 note, against 43 FRs. Three blockers on a green-light-to-build PRD would normally be disqualifying, but all three are administrative and external (deadline, group rule, proposal arbeidskrav) rather than spec-internal, and the PRD says so. That is the right call for the stakes.

Where honesty thins is capacity. The document commits to 43 FRs and five hand-annotated gold sets in ten weeks, solo, and the only stated fallback is two features deep in M2.

### Findings
- **high** The cut ladder stops at M2; M1 has no internal priority (§9.2, §9.3) — §9.3 gives an explicit and well-argued order («Kuttes i denne rekkefølgen dersom tiden ikke holder: **Mapper først, deretter Minnevers**»), but M1 bundles PDF ingestion with seven tested failure states, the full Fagsamtale with a 60-case gold set measured to three thresholds, three Kvalitetssikrede språk requiring FR-28's 30 texts plus external spot checks per language, Øvekort, Leseinnstillinger and all of §5's accessibility work — with nothing marked droppable and no order. This is the block most likely to slip, because it is the block where every item is new and measured. If M1 runs long the author has no prepared decision and will make it at 2am in November, which is exactly the failure §9.4 was written to prevent. *Fix:* rank M1 internally and name the pre-authorised retreat. The obvious one is already built into the document: FR-28 lets a language be demoted to Modellstøttet on failure, so M1 can ship *one* Kvalitetssikret språk — arabisk, since it is the one that «tvinger fram høyre-til-venstre-gjengivelse» per §4.7 and is the only one §9.5 step 8 actually demos — with ukrainsk and polsk as Modellstøttede. That is a two-thirds reduction in the heaviest measurement item, costs nothing in demonstrated capability, and is consistent with the PRD's own rule that «Nivået følger målingen, ikke ambisjonen.»
- **medium** Gold-set text sourcing is unaccounted for (FR-8, FR-14, FR-21, FR-28, FR-31, FR-40) — the document specifies nine Tekster (FR-8), five (FR-14), five (FR-21), ten per Kvalitetssikret språk (FR-28, so 30) and 20 generated verses (FR-31), but never says whether these text sets overlap. Read as disjoint that is 44 sourced ungdomstrinn texts to find, clear for copyright, and annotate; read as nested it is nine plus 30. §6.4 flags the measurement runs as «en budsjettpost, ikke en gratis bieffekt» for API cost but not for the author's hours, which are the scarcer resource. FR-40 requires all of it in the repo. *Fix:* one sentence stating the intended nesting — e.g. FR-14's and FR-21's five are a named subset of FR-8's nine, and FR-28's ten per language reuse those nine plus one. This also strengthens the per-fag reporting in FR-8, since the same texts then carry across measurements.

## Downstream usability — adequate

This PRD is chain-top and says so: the addendum opens with «Nedstrøms hører innholdet til i løsningsarkitektur og UX-spesifikasjon», and the addendum already sketches a datamodell keyed to FR numbers. So this dimension carries full weight rather than being discounted.

The foundations are right. §3's Ordliste is present, 18 terms, and the enforcement note is unusually direct: «Synonymer er en disiplinbrist, ikke en stilistisk variasjon.» The vocabulary mostly holds — Kjerneløype, Begrepssett, Kildeavsnitt, Svarvurdering, Faguttrykk and Morsmål are used identically across §4, the UJs and §10's SM definitions, and the terms are defined with extraction in mind (Faguttrykk's definition carries both inclusion examples and three exclusion classes, which is exactly what a prompt author needs). Sections pull out alone: each §4.x has its own Beskrivelse establishing context, cross-references go by FR ID or Glossary term rather than "see above", and §10 maps every SM to the FRs it validates. All four UJs have a named protagonist carrying context inline (Jonas 10. klasse, Amina two years in Norway) and each is claimed by at least one §4.x «Realiserer» line — no floating UJs, no orphan sections.

What degrades it is a cluster of resolution failures an extraction pass would hit: two broken FR cross-references in measurement requirements, one FR whose title and body disagree about scope, a PRD/addendum contradiction about whether a Tekst stays editable, an unbounded context volume for Mappe-level generation, and §4.11's documentation requirements sharing a namespace with product requirements.

### Findings
- **medium** §4.11's documentation requirements share the FR namespace with product requirements (FR-38–FR-43) — §0 justifies treating documentation as «en funksjonsseksjon med egne krav (§4.11), på linje med resten», and for planning purposes that is right. But FR-42's consequences are «Eierskap og lisens til KI-generert kode er drøftet» and FR-43's are «Refleksjonen bruker de faktiske tallene fra FR-21», which are paragraphs in a report, not software. A story-generation pass reading §4 uniformly will emit implementation stories for six requirements that have no code, and a sprint tracker will show them as un-implemented features through December. *Fix:* renumber §4.11 as DOK-1–DOK-6 (or keep the numbers and add a one-line marker: «Kravene i §4.11 er dokumentasjonsleveranser, ikke kodeoppgaver»), and update the references in §8.1, §10 SM-6 and §9's milestones. Note FR-41 is genuinely mixed — its test and code-review clauses *are* implementation work — so it may be worth splitting.
- **medium** FR-27's title and body disagree about what the feature covers (§1, FR-27, UJ-2) — the heading is «Utpeking av språklige bilder og vanskelige fagbegreper» and §1 promises «vanskelige fagbegreper og språklige bilder pekes ut spesifikt», but the first consequence scopes the set to the opposite: «atskilt fra Begrepssettet: den fanger metaforer, idiomer og faste uttrykk … som ikke er Faguttrykk». So either FR-27 also produces an L2-specific set of hard subject terms beyond the Begrepssett — in which case it needs its own extraction rule, its own density accounting, and a relationship to FR-9's cap — or it produces only figurative language and the title and §1 overpromise. UJ-2 reads the second way («Tre språklige bilder i teksten er pekt ut spesielt»). *Fix:* decide, and align the heading with the body. The narrow reading is the defensible one for v1 and is what the consequences already specify; retitle to «Utpeking av språklige bilder» and adjust §1.
- **medium** PRD and addendum disagree about whether an approved Tekst stays editable (FR-3, FR-36, addendum §3) — FR-3 makes editing a gate before the Kjerneløype starts, and no FR grants editing afterwards. The addendum assumes otherwise: «Kildeavsnitt lagres som tegnindeks inn i `redigert_tekst`, ikke som kopiert tekst — da holder henvisningen om teksten redigeres.» Those are different products. If post-approval editing exists, then character-indexed Kildeavsnitt shift under every stored Quiz question, Begrepssett entry and Fagsamtale turn, and FR-36's «Genererte elementer regenereres ikke ved gjenbesøk» guarantees the stale anchors are the ones the student sees — which silently breaks §5's «Ingen KI-utdata uten kilde» and FR-15's «Feil svar viser Kildeavsnittet der det riktige svaret står». *Fix:* state in FR-3 or FR-36 that Teksten is immutable after godkjenning (simplest, and consistent with FR-11's caching rationale), or add an invalidation rule specifying what is regenerated on edit and what the student is told. Then correct the addendum's justification, which currently argues for indices on grounds the PRD does not support.
- **medium** Two unresolvable cross-references to the Promptregister (FR-8, FR-21) — FR-8 says «Målingen kjøres på nytt for hver versjon i Promptregisteret (FR-40)» and FR-21 says «Alle tre målingene kjøres per promptversjon (FR-40)». Promptregisteret is FR-39; FR-40 is «Testsett og måleresultater i repoet». Both appear in measurement requirements, which is where a downstream reader is most likely to follow the pointer, and the error is consistent in both places (suggesting an FR was removed without renumbering — see Mechanical notes). *Fix:* change both to FR-39.
- **medium** Context volume for Mappe-level generation is unspecified (FR-32, FR-33, FR-34) — FR-32 permits eight Tekster and FR-1 permits 25 000 characters each, so FR-34's «Alle krav i §4.6 gjelder uendret, med Mappens samlede innhold som tekstgrunnlag» can mean 200 000 characters as the grounding for every Svarvurdering and every follow-up question in a six-round conversation, on the most expensive model in the project. §6.4 cites the mappe cap as a cost control without doing that arithmetic, and open question 8 asks about *code* reuse («Hvor mye av fagsamtalen kan gjenbrukes til mappesamtalen») rather than about volume. This is the one place where a requirement as written may be architecturally unbuildable inside the stated budget. *Fix:* state the strategy at requirement level — most likely per-turn selection of one or two Tekster with the Kildeavsnitt requirement pinning provenance (which FR-34's «Hvert Samtalespørsmål viser hvilken Tekst det er forankret i» already implies) — or lower the Mappe cap for samtale specifically. Worth adding to open question 8.
- **low** Glossary drift on «fagbegrep» — §3 defines **Faguttrykk**, but «fagbegrep(er)» appears as a near-synonym in §1 («det norske fagbegrepet står alltid sammen med oversettelsen»), in UJ-1 («fjorten fagbegreper markert»), in UJ-2, in FR-27's title, and — most consequentially — as the name of a threshold in FR-28 and SM-4: «**Bevaring av fagbegrep:** minst **90 prosent** av Faguttrykkene …», where the label and the measured object use different words in the same sentence. *Fix:* rename the FR-28 metric to «Bevaring av Faguttrykk» and use Faguttrykk in §1 and the UJs; UJ prose can keep it informal if §3 lists it as a non-normative synonym.

## Shape fit — strong

No findings beyond the §4.11 namespace issue already recorded under downstream usability.

The shape matches the product on every axis I checked.
